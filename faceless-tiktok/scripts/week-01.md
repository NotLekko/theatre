## 003 · You're using ChatGPT wrong (the 4-part fix)
**Pillar:** ✍️ Prompt Fix · **Hook text:** "You're using ChatGPT wrong" · **Keywords:** chatgpt tips, how to prompt, chatgpt hacks

### Voiceover
> You're using ChatGPT wrong, and it's why the answers feel so generic.
> Watch. I asked it to "write a cover letter," and I got this. Bland, too long, and it sounds like everyone else's.
> The fix has four parts. I call it R C T F.
> R is for Role. Tell it who to be. "You're a hiring manager at a design agency."
> C is for Context. Give it what it can't guess. The job post, your experience, and what makes you different.
> T is for Task. Be specific. "Write a cover letter under two hundred words that opens with a result I achieved."
> F is for Format. Tell it how you want it back. "Three short paragraphs, no clichés, end with a question."
> Same tool, same thirty seconds. Now look at the difference. It's shorter, it's specific to me, and it actually sounds like a person wrote it.
> This works in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, any of them.
> Bonus: finish every prompt with "ask me any questions you need before you start." It interviews you first, and the answer gets even better.
> Follow, because tomorrow I'll give you the exact script that gets your internet bill lowered.
