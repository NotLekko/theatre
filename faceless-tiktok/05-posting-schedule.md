# 05 — Posting Schedule

Times are **US Eastern (ET)**, because US views pay the highest Creator Rewards RPM. If your audience turns out mostly UK-based, use the same clock times in UK time.

## The plan in phases

| Phase | Days | Posts/day | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Warm-up** | 1–3 | 0 | Train your For You page, build trust, batch-produce Week 1 |
| **Test** | 4–17 | 1 | Post scripts #001–#014. Test two time slots and five pillars. |
| **Scale** | 18–30 | 2 | One **anchor** (over 60s, earns Rewards) + one **reach cut** (25–40s, grows followers) |
| **Compound** (Month 2+) | 31+ | 2–3 | Double down on the winning pillar; start cross-posting (see 06) |

Why these numbers: new accounts do best with roughly 1–2 posts a day while they test formats, and Buffer's analysis of 11.4M posts found real lifts from 2–5 posts a week upward. More than 3 a day from a new account mostly adds burnout. **Consistency beats volume.** Never go a day with zero posts in the first 60 days.

## Time slots

Industry data disagrees: Sprout Social favours Tue–Thu 2–6 PM, RecurPost's data on 2M+ posts favours Mon 1 PM, Fri 8 PM and Sat 8 PM, and Buffer favours Sun 9 AM. So during the test phase we check your own audience:

| Slot | Time (ET) | Used on |
|---|---|---|
| **A** (lunch) | 1:00 PM | Alternate weekdays |
| **B** (evening) | 7:00 PM | Alternate weekdays |
| **W** (weekend) | 10:00 AM | Sat & Sun |

On **Day 17**, compare the median 48-hour views of the five A posts against the five B posts. The winner becomes your anchor slot, and the loser becomes the reach-cut slot. Keep posts **at least 4 hours apart**.

**Total in 30 days: 40 videos** (27 anchors + 13 reach cuts).

**Reach cuts (R01–R13)** are 25–40 second recuts of *your own* best videos. Use a new hook, show only the single most useful step, and close with "full version on my page." They're too short for Creator Rewards, but they're cheap follower magnets. Because it's your own footage, it isn't "reused content."

**Seasonal hooks coming up:** Halloween (Oct 31) · US Election Day (Nov 3; stay non-political) · Black Friday / Cyber Monday (Nov 27–30; **price history #013 remix!**) · New Year budgeting (Jan). Plan these 2 weeks ahead.

## Weekly rhythm (~8–10 hours/week for 7–14 videos)

| When | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday | Pick topics (backlog + TikTok search autocomplete + what's new in AI this week); write/refresh scripts; run the length checker | 2 h |
| Sunday/Monday | Generate all voiceovers; record all demos in one sitting | 2 h |
| Mon–Tue | Edit the whole batch from the master template | 4–5 h |
| Tuesday | Covers, captions, hashtags; schedule the week in TikTok Studio (web) | 45 min |
| **Daily** | First hour after each post: reply to comments, pin the prompt, send 1 video reply | 15 min |

## What to measure (TikTok Studio → Analytics, 48h after posting)

| Metric | Where the problem is | Rule of thumb |
|---|---|---|
| Retention in the first 2s | **Hook** | If you lose more than ~35% of viewers in the first 2 seconds, rewrite the hook |
| Average watch time | **Pacing / demo** | Aim for ≥ 50% of the video's length |
| % watched full video | **Payoff / length** | 15%+ on a ~70s video is solid |
| Shares + saves per 1K views | **Value** | 10+ means the content is useful; money videos should beat this |
| Profile views → follows | **Series appeal** | Low? Make the series tag and the "tomorrow" tease stronger |
| Search traffic % | **SEO** | Rising over time = keyword captions are working |

## Decision rules

1. **Big drop in the first 2 seconds** → same video, new hook. Re-post it as a remix after 7 days.
2. **Drop in the middle** → the demo is too slow. More zooms, tighter cuts, remove a step.
3. **Great retention, low views** → it's the topic or the search terms, not the video. Retitle, re-keyword, or angle it at a trend.
4. **Hits 2× your median views** → make 3 follow-ups this week (new hook, part 2, reach cut).
5. **A pillar has the lowest median after 6+ videos** → cut its share by 10% and give it to the top pillar.
6. **Don't delete flops.** Leave them up and learn from them.
7. **Day 30 review:** total followers, total views, top 5 videos, winning pillar, winning slot. Then rewrite the Month 2 mix and decide whether to go to 3 posts a day.
