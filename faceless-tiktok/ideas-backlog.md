# Ideas Backlog — Shortcuts #015+

Turn these into full scripts using the format in [03](03-content-playbook.md#script-format) and the examples in [`scripts/`](scripts/). Check each with `python3 faceless-tiktok/tools/script_length.py`.

**Every idea must pass the 3-question test:**
1. Would someone **search** for this?
2. Can I **show** it working on screen in ~60 seconds?
3. Does it save a **measurable** amount of time or money?

🔎 = check the feature still exists and works as described before scripting (tools change fast).

## Where to find new ideas every week

- **TikTok search autocomplete.** Type "how to … with ai", "chatgpt…", "save money on…" and note what completes.
- **Your own comments.** Every "can it do X?" is a video, and a video reply is the fastest version.
- **This week's AI launches.** Product Hunt, "There's An AI For That", AI newsletters. Be early, and be honest if it's hype.
- **Reddit "how do I" posts** in r/personalfinance, r/productivity, r/ChatGPT, r/Frugal.
- **Your swipe file** (02): remake the *structure* of hooks that stopped your thumb, never the content.
