# 06 — Monetization, ROI & Rules

## Money roadmap by milestone

| Milestone | Unlocks | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Affiliate programs | Apply to the affiliate programs of AI tools you **actually use** in videos. Many run through PartnerStack or Impact, or the tool's own "Affiliates" page. Prefer **recurring** commissions. |
| **Week 3** | Email list | Launch the freebie **"The Shortcut Vault"**: a Notion page with every prompt from every video, gated by email (beehiiv or Kit, free tiers). Every "comment PROMPT" becomes a reason to join. |
| **~1K followers** | Bio link (follower minimum applies to personal accounts; check TikTok Studio) | Link-in-bio page, in this order: ① Free Shortcut Vault ② Tools I use (affiliate) ③ Product (later). Until then, add your Instagram/YouTube to the profile where available and put the link there. |
| **~5K followers** | Sponsor interest; TikTok Shop (optional) | Build a one-page media kit: niche, audience, median views, 3 best videos. Optional: a TikTok Shop showcase for desk and tech gear. |
| **10K followers + 100K views/30 days** | **Creator Rewards** | TikTok Studio → Monetization → apply. Only 60s+ anchors earn, which is why every anchor is ~70s. |
| **~500 email subs** | Digital product | **"The Shortcut Pack"** ($9–19): 100 tested prompts + the budget sheet (#011) + meal-plan and week-plan templates. Near 100% margin. |
| **~10K+ engaged followers** | Paid sponsorships | Pitch AI tool startups. Price from your **median views**, not follower count. Early deals are commonly a few hundred dollars per video. |
| **Month 2+** | Cross-posting | Post the same videos (exported **without** the TikTok watermark) to YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. It's extra reach and extra revenue for zero extra production. |

## ROI model (illustrative, not a promise)

**Formulas**
- Creator Rewards = monthly views × 70% (share of views on 60s+ anchors) × 50% (share that qualify) × RPM ($0.80 tech/US blend) ÷ 1,000
- Affiliate = monthly views × 0.15% bio clicks × 30% tool clicks × 3% paid signups × $20 average commission
- Product = bio clicks × 30% email sign-ups × 1% buyers per month × $15
- Sponsors = deals per month × fee

**Month 6 scenarios**

| | Conservative | Base | Optimistic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly views | 500K | 2M | 8M |
| Creator Rewards | ~$140 | ~$560 | ~$2,240 |
| Affiliate | ~$135 | ~$540 | ~$2,160 |
| Digital product | ~$35 | ~$135 | ~$540 |
| Sponsors | $0 | ~$500 | ~$2,000 |
| **Monthly revenue** | **~$310** | **~$1,735** | **~$6,940** |
| Monthly tool costs | ~$30 | ~$40 | ~$60 |

Recurring affiliate commissions **stack**: a signup from month 3 still pays in month 6. That's why the pillar mix is 25% Tool Drop.

**Reality check:** Month 1 revenue is ≈ $0. Plan for 60–90 days of consistent posting before real traction; most faceless accounts fail by quitting before then. These numbers are illustrative assumptions built on creator-reported benchmarks, not a forecast.

## Rules that keep the account (and the money) safe

**Disclosure**
- Affiliate links: say or show "link in bio — I may earn a commission," and add `#ad` or "affiliate" to the caption. The FTC requires clear, upfront disclosure.
- Sponsored videos: turn on TikTok's **Content disclosure** toggle (Brand/branded content) every time. It's mandatory for promotional posts.
- Synthetic voice or AI visuals: turn on the **AI-generated content** label (see 04).

**Money & legal content**
- Consumer savings only (bills, subscriptions, refunds, shopping). **No investing, stocks, crypto or "buy this."** This keeps you out of finfluencer rules.
- Add "Not financial advice" / "Not legal advice" to captions where relevant (#009, #012).
- Never invent numbers. Show your own demo results or cite a source on screen.

**Originality (protects Creator Rewards)**
- Every video uses your own screen recording, your own script and your own voice. Never re-upload other creators' clips.
- Only use music from TikTok's or CapCut's licensed libraries on monetized videos.
- Don't post identical videos from multiple TikTok accounts.

**Privacy**
- Blur emails, names, addresses and account numbers in every recording.
- Remove personal data from files *before* uploading them to any AI tool (and tell viewers to do the same).

**Admin**
- Creator income is taxable. Track revenue and tool costs in a simple sheet from Day 1. The #011 budget sheet works.
- Only promote tools you've actually used on camera. Trust is the asset.
