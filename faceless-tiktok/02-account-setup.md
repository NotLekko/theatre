### Why "Tool Theory"

- **Sounds like a media brand**, not a hack account ("Hard Fork", "The Verge", "Morning Brew" territory). Sponsors take it seriously.
- **Says the niche:** *tools* = what we cover; *theory* = we explain *why* it works, not just "10 AI tools you NEED."
- **Room to grow:** it covers AI now and any tech later, without a rename.
- **Easy to say** in a voiceover, alliterative, and it works on every platform.
- **No conflicts found** in a Sept 2026 web search (no AI/tech brand, channel or product with that name). "Toolwise" (taken by an AI tools review site), "Byte Brief" and "Clearstack" (both in use) were rejected, and "The AI Shortcut" sat too close to *AI Shortcut Lab* and the *Shortcut* Excel AI tool.
- Before committing, run a quick check on [USPTO trademark search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/) and grab the domain (e.g. `tooltheory.co` / `.ai`) if you want one.

## Setup checklist (Day 1, ~1 hour)

- [ ] Create a **new Gmail** just for the brand. Use it for TikTok, ElevenLabs, affiliate programs and the email list.
- [ ] Install TikTok on your **phone** and sign up there, not on desktop.
- [ ] **Keep it a Personal account.** Creator Rewards only pays personal accounts. Don't switch to Business.
- [ ] Use your real birthday (must be 18+ for Creator Rewards).
- [ ] Set username, display name, bio and profile photo (above).
- [ ] **Settings → Privacy:** account **Public**; "Suggest your account to others" **on**; comments **on**; Duet/Stitch **on** (free reach); video downloads **off** (tutorials get re-uploaded by content thieves).
- [ ] **Settings → Security:** turn on 2-step verification.
- [ ] Don't use a VPN, and don't log into several TikTok accounts on this phone. Both are common reasons new accounts get suppressed.
- [ ] Confirm you live in a Creator Rewards-eligible country (US, UK, DE, FR, JP, KR, BR, MX and others; check in TikTok Studio → Monetization).

## Warm-up (Days 1–3, ~25 min/day)

New accounts that post aggressively on day one often get throttled. Spend three days acting like a real viewer while you batch-produce Week 1 (see 05):

- [ ] Search your keywords: `ai tools`, `chatgpt tips`, `ai hacks`, `save money hacks`, `productivity apps`. Watch the top results **all the way through**.
- [ ] Follow 20–30 accounts in the niche. Like, save and leave **real** comments (a full sentence, not "🔥").
- [ ] Keep a **swipe file** (a Notes doc) of every hook that stopped your thumb. Write down the first line and why it worked.
- [ ] Day 3: post nothing yet. On Day 4 you start posting (see 05).
